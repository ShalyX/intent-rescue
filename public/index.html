01 / THE PROBLEMSTATUS VS EXPLANATION

A transaction status isn't an explanation.

A swap can report failure while the evidence tells a more complicated story. The useful question isn't just what status appeared; it's which part of the transfer can actually be established.

A missing destination record does not tell you where the funds went. A missing refund record does not prove a refund happened.

04 / THE EVIDENCE STANDARDNO GUESSWORK

Known, supported,
and unknown are different.

OSIALI keeps the evidence language visible throughout an investigation. A confident-looking status is not a substitute for a source that supports it.

OBSERVED

Direct identifier or transaction evidence is present in the evidence set.

INFERRED

Supported by related evidence, but not directly observed.

UNKNOWN

No evidence observed. No conclusion is drawn.

THE BOUNDARY MATTERSMissing evidence is not proof of loss, protocol fault, or refund.

Public Explorer snapshots can be stale and are not independent chain-finality verification. User-supplied evidence remains provisional until checked against original sources.
